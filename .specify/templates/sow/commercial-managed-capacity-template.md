## Capacity Guardrails

- Commitments are framed as capacity, roles, cadence, and operating model unless
  source material explicitly defines fixed deliverables.
- Backlog priority, acceptance, and tradeoff decisions must have a named client
  owner.
- Unused capacity, overflow demand, after-hours work, specialist roles, or new
  fixed deliverables require explicit handling in the SOW or change process.
- Incidents, service requests, advisory questions, enhancements, and new
  engagement candidates should be classified separately before capacity is
  consumed.
- Enhancement work that adds new users, integrations, data domains,
  environments, compliance obligations, production dependencies, or material
  delivery effort must route to a new spec, change order, or new engagement.
- Rates, fees, investment, and payment terms remain in external commercial
  artifacts. Generated SOW value cells always use `TBD - Commercial Review`.
